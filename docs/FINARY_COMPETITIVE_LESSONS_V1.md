# Finary Competitive Lessons V1

**Date:** 2026-05-01  
**Target Competitor:** Finary  
**Objective:** Learn from Finary's dominance in wealth aggregation while differentiating through Moneyflow's unique Intelligence and Social layers.

---

## 1. What Finary Does Well

- **Wealth Aggregation:** Seamless integration of banks, brokers, crypto exchanges, and real estate.
- **Multi-Asset Tracking:** Supports niche assets like private equity, watches, and physical gold.
- **Connector Trust:** Excellent UX around "Read-Only" connections and bank-level data security messaging.
- **Performance Reports:** Clear visualizations of portfolio growth, dividends, and asset allocation.
- **Pricing Strategy:** Successful high-price annual premium model ($100+/year) targeting high-net-worth users.
- **Community & Content:** Strong fly-wheel of financial education, community discussions, and product updates.

---

## 2. What Moneyflow Must Match (Baseline Standards)

- **Net Worth Clarity:** Instant, accurate calculation of total net worth across all currencies and categories.
- **Connected Account Trust:** Transparent "Connection Hub" showing sync status, provider names, and "Disconnect" options.
- **Asset/Liability Coverage:** Native support for the "Big 4" (Cash, Stocks, Crypto, Real Estate).
- **Sync States:** Clear loading/syncing indicators and helpful error messages for expired bank tokens.
- **Privacy Controls:** Mandatory 2FA options and explicit data-deletion workflows.

---

## 3. What Moneyflow Can Do Differently (The Differentiators)

- **Tricount-style Groups:** Integrated shared expenses that automatically calculate "personal share" within the net worth view.
- **Ask Moneyflow AI:** A conversational "Neural Core" that answers questions like *"How much did I spend on restaurants this month compared to last?"*
- **Palantir 11 Signals:** A unique intelligence layer mapping external geopolitical and macro events to the user's personal wealth profile.
- **Mass-Market Entry:** A simpler, "Budgetting-first" entry promise ("Capisci i tuoi soldi") compared to Finary's "Investor-first" branding.
- **Shared Expenses Impact:** Seeing how group debts and settlements affect your actual liquidity in real-time.
- **World Context:** Not just *what* you have, but *why* your assets might be moving based on world news.

## 5. Trust Copy Lessons

**Safe Moneyflow Copy:**
- *"We have read-only access to your data. We can never move your money."*
- *"Your credentials are encrypted and never stored on our servers."*
- *"All financial data is processed within European infrastructure (GDPR compliant)."*
- *"Neural Core uses a sanitized, redacted version of your data to provide insights."*
- *"Moneyflow is an orientation tool. We do not provide regulated financial advice."*

**Avoid:**
- "Bank-grade security" (Too vague/unsupported).
- "Guaranteed returns".
- "We will optimize your investments".
- "Institutional-grade wealth management".

---

## 6. Pricing Lessons (Hypotheses)

- **Free (Entry):** Manual tracking, 1 Group, Basic Dashboard. Target: Mass Market.
- **Premium (Wealth Intelligence):** Bank Sync, AI Advisor, Palantir Awareness, Unlimited Groups. Target: Power Users.
- **Family/Groups:** Share premium features across multiple accounts in a shared circle.
- **Pro:** Advanced CSV exports, Multi-currency holdings, Custom Palantir focus. Target: Freelancers/Investors.

---

## 7. Differentiation Statement

**“Finary helps users track their wealth. Moneyflow helps users understand their money, coordinate shared expenses, ask questions, and monitor global signals that may affect their financial life.”**
